How to use FarmWise AI

  1. Upload a scheme document

    Go to the 'Analyze Scheme' tab and upload a PDF file of the government scheme you want to understand.

  2. Read the summary

    The AI will create an easy-to-understand summary of the scheme, explaining benefits and requirements.

  3. Listen to the audio

    If you prefer, you can listen to an audio summary in your chosen language.

  4. Check eligibility

    Answer the questions to see if you qualify for the scheme.

  5. Save schemes

    Save important schemes to your list for future reference.

  6. Change language

    Use the sidebar to select your preferred language.
